I teach graduate and undergraduate courses at [Télécom Saint-Étienne](https://www.telecom-st-etienne.fr/) (2023 – present).

---

### Courses

**Semantic Search & Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — *Graduate elective*  
Embedding models, dense retrieval, re-ranking, RAG pipelines with LLMs. Students build end-to-end systems over domain-specific corpora. I also cover evaluation: when retrieval is the bottleneck vs. generation.

**Machine Learning** — *Graduate*  
Classification, clustering, ensemble methods, model selection. Focus on the gap between theory and deployment: what the math says vs. what actually fails in practice.

**Natural Language Processing** — *Graduate*  
Text representations, sequence modeling, transformers, applied NLP pipelines. Emphasis on low-resource and domain-adaptation scenarios.

**Data Structures & Algorithms** — *Undergraduate, C/C++*  
Complexity, sorting, trees, graphs, dynamic programming. Taught in C and C++ with a focus on memory and correctness.

**Numerical Methods** — *Undergraduate*  
Numerical integration, root-finding, linear systems. Bridging the gap between mathematical definitions and floating-point reality.
